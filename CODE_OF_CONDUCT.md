# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation in this project respectful and harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about technical problems without attacking people.
- Give reproducible examples and actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy: never publish another person's screenshots, credentials or data.
- Accept correction and focus on resolving the issue.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information,
and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions or block participation when behavior
violates this policy. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
